## 🖥️ First Launch and a Quick Tour

When Shotcut opens for the first time, you will see a working area with several panels. Nothing is broken if the layout looks busy; each panel has a job.

- The player panel shows your video as you scrub through it.
- The timeline sits at the bottom and holds your clips in order.
- The playlist and file panels on the side let you collect media before placing it on the timeline.
- The properties and filter panels let you adjust the selected clip.

A simple first project helps you learn the layout:

1. Create a new project and give it a name.
2. Bring a short video file into the playlist.
3. Drag that file onto the timeline.
4. Press play to preview it.
5. Use the razor or split tool to cut the clip at a point you choose.
6. Delete the section you do not want.
7. Export the result as a new video file.

Repeat this process a few times with short clips. Familiarity with the timeline is the single biggest step toward comfortable editing.

## 💡 Tips for Smoother Editing

- Keep your source files in one folder so you can find them again.
- Save your project regularly, especially before applying heavy filters.
- Use short test exports to confirm settings before exporting a long video.
- Close other demanding applications while editing 4K footage.
- Work with footage from a local drive rather than a removable drive when possible.
- Preview at a lower quality setting if playback stutters, then export at full quality.
